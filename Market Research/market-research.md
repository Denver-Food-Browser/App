### Estimated visits from organic search
Peak Monthly Load = (1.35 to 1.50) x Total yearly load / 12
- Assuming a maximum total yearly load of all people experiencing food insecurity in Denver metro, this will max out at approximately **60,000**/month

Peak daily load = (1.5 to 1.8) x Total monthly load / 30.5

Peak hourly load = (1.4 to 1.6) x Total daily load / Average work hours
- Peak hourly load of full scale: ~483

### Realistic scalability benchmarks
1. **Initial testing**: 10 people, make sure it works for internal team members on various devices in various contexts
2. **Initial external rollout**: Send this to 100 people in the community that can use it and test it for a week or two and report any issues.
3. **External rollout**: Share more broadly with the community. Based on several sources it is reasonable for early adoption of this product to be around 1% of total market, so round 600 in monthly visitors
4. **Growth to 10% of the market**: If things go well, we will grow to 10% of the total market within the first few years, so 6,000 in monthly traffic and 48.3 in peak hourly traffic.
